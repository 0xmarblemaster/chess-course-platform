How we help a child learn and move forward

Will my child find it interesting?

Our programme has a built-in motivation system that keeps children interested and eager to keep going. We want lessons to be both useful and fun.

Every new student gets a book on our method as a gift.

Will the lessons be effective?

The programme lets each child move at their own pace. Those who learn faster aren’t held back. Those who need more time get help understanding and consolidating the basics. Our method accounts for these differences within the group.

Will my child get attention?

Our groups have no more than 10 students. The coach gives attention to each one: helps with exercises, answers questions and checks understanding.

Who will teach my child?

Chess Empire has its own coach academy. In training we put teaching skills first: the ability to explain, to engage and to work with children. We want our coaches to be able to pass their knowledge on to a child.

Frequently asked questions

How much do lessons cost?

The price depends on the branch and the plan. Basic plans start at 29 500 ₸/month for 2 lessons a week of 60 minutes. At the Gagarin Park and Debut branches, from 35 900 ₸/month. All plans and discounts are in the “Branches & prices” section.

Which branches have Kazakh-speaking coaches?

Kazakh-speaking coaches work at the Debut, Zhandosov and Almaty Arena branches.

Do you offer private lessons?

Private lessons are only given as a trial lesson or when preparing for major competitions (10 000 ₸/hour). For beginners and continuing students we recommend group lessons — they work better for development.

Can my child attend once a week?

No, we don’t offer that format. Once a week doesn’t give enough results or practice. Progress needs at least 2 lessons a week.

How do tournaments work and when can my child take part?

Tournaments are held every weekend at 5 branches. A child can take part after completing the first stage of training and passing an exam with their coach. The membership includes up to 4 free tournaments a month.

Do you teach adults?

Yes, adult classes are held at the Debut branch (71 Kabanbay Batyr St) from 19:00 to 21:00. One lesson costs 5 000 ₸. The trial lesson is free.

How does the personal account work?

The personal account is set up with the coach after the first lesson. We’ll help you register and explain how to use it.

Let’s get acquainted

At the trial lesson the coach gets to know your child, assesses their level and gives them their first exercises. Afterwards we’ll recommend a suitable group and answer your questions.
